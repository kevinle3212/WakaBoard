# Privacy

WakaBoard has no telemetry, ads, analytics SDK, backend, or runtime
third-party dependency. It requests read-only WakaTime analytics, stores
credentials in Keychain, and caches normalized analytics locally. Widgets use
minimal cached aggregates and never receive credentials. Widget placeholders
contain no personal data. Clear cache and logout controls are provided by the
app; operating-system backups and widget surfaces remain the user’s choice.
